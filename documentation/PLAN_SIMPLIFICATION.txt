════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
RÈGLES DE SIMPLIFICATION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. LABELS:
   Avant: (gen-label 'LOOP_START)
   Après: (gen-label-simplified "LOOP_START")

2. SETF:
   Avant: (setf (accessor obj) val)
   Après: (setq obj (set-accessor obj val))
   
3. CAR/CDR:
   Avant: (car (cdr list))
   Après: Passer éléments séparément ou utiliser FIRST/SECOND/etc.

4. EQ/LISTP:
   Avant: (eq x y) ou (listp x)
   Après: (= (get-id x) (get-id y)) ou tests alternatifs

5. LOOP WHILE:
   Avant: (loop while cond do ...)
   Après: (defun loop-helper () (if cond (progn ... (loop-helper)) nil))

6. APPEND multiple:
   Avant: (append a b c d)
   Après: (append-two a (append-two b (append-two c d)))

7. SETQ complexe:
   Avant: (setq (car x) val)
   Après: Ne pas utiliser, restructurer
